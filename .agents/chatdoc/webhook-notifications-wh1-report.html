The mechanism in one paragraph

Tap, not truth. The AI2 audit event feed stays the resumable source of truth; webhooks are a low-latency push tap. An in-process WebhookDispatcher follows audit_events through the engine's existing readAuditEvents keyset read — read-only, no engine command, no TLA+ action, no projection change — and POSTs matching events to operator-registered URLs, signed with a per-subscription HMAC secret. Per-subscription durable cursor: restarts resume where delivery stopped; exhausted deliveries stay recoverable through the feed.

Delivery semantics (normative summary)

  • Ordered, at-least-once, head-of-line blocking per subscription; a failure stops that subscription's page, other subscriptions proceed.
  • Retry at 10 s → 50 s → ~4 m → ~21 m; after 5 attempts the delivery is exhausted (visible in GET …/deliveries), cursor advances, feed remains the recovery path.
  • Body is exactly the feed Event shape; headers X-DumpLedger-Event, -Delivery, -Delivered-At, -Signature-256, User-Agent: DumpLedger-Webhook/1. Receiver verifies: HMAC_SHA256(secret, raw body), constant-time compare.
